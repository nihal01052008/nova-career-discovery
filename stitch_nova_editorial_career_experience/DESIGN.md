## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an avant-garde editorial presence designed to empower emerging creative minds and ambitious students. Rejecting conventional career-portal corporate aesthetics, it balances high-fashion publication gravitas with the surgical precision of Swiss typography and modern hardware-level polish. 

The aesthetic is High-Contrast Editorial Brutalism tempered by refined minimalism: razor-sharp lines, deliberate structural rules, monumental display forms, and disciplined negative space. The atmosphere evokes the tension of a contemporary gallery catalogue coupled with an ultra-responsive creative workshop. It delivers confidence, intellectual stimulation, and cultural cachet without alienating functional usability.

## Colors

The palette operates on stark, high-contrast visual tension:
- **Base Canvas (`neutral_color_hex`: #F9F8F6)**: A warm gallery ivory providing human warmth, physical paper authenticity, and eliminating digital glare. Paired with a tonal container shade (`#F4F2ED`) and surface elevation (`#ECEAE3`).
- **Primary Ink (`primary_color_hex`: #111111)**: Deep architectural carbon that anchors typography, borders, and dominant structural wireframes. An elevated secondary charcoal (`#18181B`) serves as contextual depth for cards and contrasting surfaces.
- **Signal Lime (`secondary_color_hex`: #D4FF00)**: An electric hyper-chromatic acid lime used sparingly for focal actions, active system states, micro-badges, kinetic highlights, and directional focus.
- **Deep Cobalt (`tertiary_color_hex`: #254EDB)**: A disciplined blue that balances the acidity of the lime, used for informational badges, verified institutional seals, and editorial emphasis.

Use hair-thin primary borders (`#111111` at 12–20% opacity or solid 1px borders) to divide editorial zones. Keep the lime reserved for high-impact intentional points of focus; never dilute its impact across large background areas.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs three distinct voices:
1. **Syne (Display & Headlines)**: A sculptural, art-forward geometric typeface with wide proportions and radical letterforms. It anchors big editorial statements and section heads.
2. **Space Grotesk (Body & Content)**: A proportional, technical grotesque that maintains Swiss clarity with subtle idiosyncratic mechanical quirks suited for readability.
3. **JetBrains Mono (Metadata & Micro-Labels)**: A monospaced typeface reserved for indexing, timestamps, category tags, system status indicators, and coordinate markers.

Editorial treatment rules:
- Set all `label-*` tiers in uppercase with positive tracking.
- Apply tight letter-spacing to large Syne headlines to establish an uncompromising poster-like silhouette.
- Maintain editorial rhythm with generous margins between headlines and body blocks.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout adheres to a disciplined 12-column architectural grid on desktop, 8-column on tablet, and 4-column on mobile.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: Max canvas container of 1440px or 100vw bounded by `grid-margin-desktop` (64px) and `gutter-desktop` (32px). Content blocks span deliberate asymmetric groupings (e.g., 5-col narrative / 7-col showcase).
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8 columns with 24px gutters and 32px side margins. Multicolumn editorial layouts collapse into balanced two-column configurations.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with 16px gutters and 20px edge margins. Layout stacks vertically with structural horizontal rules delineating modules.

Vertical rhythm relies on intentional step intervals: massive section drops (`space-4xl` and `space-5xl`) contrast against tightly indexed card grids (`space-md` and `space-lg`). Content modules are partitioned with razor-thin hairline borders rather than arbitrary white cushions.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system dispenses with soft, simulated physical drop-shadows, prioritizing a flat, tectonic surface hierarchy anchored by contrast, hairline borders, and contextual fills:
- **Level 0 (Canvas)**: Background field in warm ivory (`#F9F8F6`).
- **Level 1 (Structural Containers)**: Outlined surfaces defined by 1px solid `#111111` or `#111111` at 15% opacity, filled with surface tint `#F4F2ED`.
- **Level 2 (Active/Hover/Floating Sheets)**: High-contrast elevation achieved via hard, solid offset drop-lines (e.g., `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #111111`) or solid charcoal inversion (`#111111` background with `#F9F8F6` text and `#D4FF00` highlights).
- **Level 3 (Modal / Overlays)**: Full-bleed architectural panels backed by subtle noise texture or semi-translucent ivory frost (`rgba(249, 248, 246, 0.94)`) combined with a sharp 1px primary frame.

Depth is communicated through horizontal hairline dividers, sharp rectangular layering, and deliberate contrast flips rather than blurred illumination.

## Shapes

The design system enforces a pure, zero-radius geometry (`0px`). 

Every component—cards, badges, buttons, inputs, modal containers, and hover plates—features sharp, clean right angles. This architectural discipline reinforces the high-fashion editorial identity and honors the Swiss design lineage. 

Pill shapes and soft rounded corners are strictly prohibited. The only curved elements across the platform are deliberate graphic glyphs, kinetic circular stamps, and curved vector editorial accents.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action**: Sharp rectangular container. Solid `#111111` fill, `#F9F8F6` typography in `label-lg`, 1px solid `#111111` border. Padding: `14px 28px`. Hover: Invert fill to `#D4FF00` and typography to `#111111`, accompanied by a 2px top-left translation.
- **Secondary / Outline**: Sharp rectangular container. Transparent fill, 1px solid `#111111` border, `#111111` label text. Hover: Solid `#111111` fill with `#F9F8F6` text.
- **Accent Action**: Solid `#D4FF00` fill, 1px solid `#111111` border, `#111111` label text. Active/Focus: Hard offset shadow `2px 2px 0px #111111`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Category Chips**: Monospaced `label-md`, uppercase, 0px corner radius. Padding: `4px 8px`. Border: 1px solid `#111111` at 25% opacity. Default background: transparent.
- **Accent Badges**: `#D4FF00` fill with `#111111` text, or `#254EDB` fill with `#F9F8F6` text for verified/curated industry roles.

### Form Inputs
- **Text Fields**: Crisp 1px solid `#111111` bottom-only border or 4-sided wireframe box. Background: `#F4F2ED`. Text in `body-md`, labels in `label-sm` positioned strictly above the input. Focus state: 2px solid `#111111` border with an interior indicator tag in `#D4FF00`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: 0px square containers (18x18px) with 1.5px solid `#111111` border. Checked state: Solid `#111111` box housing an electric `#D4FF00` inner block or inverted tick.

### Cards & Grid Cells
- **Editorial Discovery Card**: Enclosed by a 1px solid `#111111` boundary line. Background: `#F4F2ED`. Layout begins with an index tracker (`[ 01 ]`, `[ DISCOVER ]`) in `label-sm`, followed by imagery with raw-cut 0px framing, `headline-sm` title, and brief metadata tags. Hover: The entire container gains a hard offset shadow (`4px 4px 0px #111111`) or switches background to `#FFFFFF`.

### Editorial Rules & Index Bars
- **Micro-rules**: 1px horizontal lines (`#111111` at 15% opacity or 100% black for main module breaks). 
- **Coordinates & Index Headers**: Running headers with dynamic counters, breadcrumbs in `label-md`, and system telemetry markers separating viewports into structured magazine spreads.
